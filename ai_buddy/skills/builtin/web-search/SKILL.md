---
name: web-search
description: Searches the web (DuckDuckGo, or a self-hosted SearXNG) and reads the top pages, or reads one page by its address. Use for anything current or uncertain - weather, news, prices, exchange rates, sports results, people, products, opening hours, events. Sends the search query to the search engine; nothing else leaves the laptop.
metadata:
  kira-label: Web search
---
- web_search for anything current or that you aren't sure of: weather, news, prices, exchange rates, sports results, people, products, opening hours, events, or anything "latest". Your own knowledge is out of date, so don't answer those from memory.
- fetch_page to read a specific page, such as a link from your search results.
